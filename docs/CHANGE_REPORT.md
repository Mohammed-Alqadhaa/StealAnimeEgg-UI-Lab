### 1. Summary
The Last CP was turned into a playable full-production place. It has:
* one fenced Farm Safe Zone with 7 farms, 9 linear worlds, 45 characters and 45 character-specific eggs;
* auto-secure, 60 s hatching, OPEN-to-reveal with real models, and an Index discovered on open;
* treadmill-only Speed with 10 visual levels, 12 exact trail tiers, and the Makima and Rem kiosks;
* bosses, a server-validated PvP bat, and 300 s rounds with a 7 s fog gate;
* persistence and the production UI from the UI Lab.

### 9. Architecture
* Rojo-style sources live in `game/src/{shared,server,client}`.
* The UI is generated by `tools/build-prod.js`, and the world is baked by the Lune assembler
  `game/assembler/build.luau`.
* There is one server entry script and one client entry script; the services are plain modules
  with an explicit boot order.

### 10. Shared configuration
* `Worlds`, `Balance` (OWNER_LOCKED values plus provisional values), `Economy`, `Layout`, `Trails`,
  `MarketplaceConfig` and `Animations`.
* Gameplay code contains no hard-coded balance numbers.

### 11. Worlds & roster
* 9 worlds in the order DS, MHA, DragonBall, OnePiece, Naruto, Berserk, Bleach, JJK, SoloLeveling,
  with 5 characters each and the boss among them.
* One Piece includes Nami and Kaido (no Sanji/Law). JJK includes Mahoraga (no Megumi).
* Aizen is **Muken** with a galaxy egg. Yhwach is **The Almighty**. Giyu is preserved but is not in
  the active 5.

### 12. Linear route
Farm Safe Zone → fog gate (z 198) → worlds from z 205 at a 260-stud pitch. `Layout.worldAt` and
`isInSafeZone` are shared by the server, the client and the tests.

### 13. World footprint = Akaza ×1.5
* Every world is 150 studs wide (walls at ±75) and 230 long, plus a 30-stud themed entry portal.
* Tall invisible colliders keep the route linear.

### 14. Demon Slayer (existing Akaza world)
* The BlenderEnvironment was moved out of the 6-zone prototype and widened by **translating**
  authored detail outward (292 parts moved by 25 studs) and **cloning** floor tiles (14).
* The walk surface grew 72 → 122 and the torii beam 70 → 120; the entrance walls were refit.
* Added: the moon and a title arch. The central arena, compass and pedestals are untouched.

### 15. Other 8 worlds
* Original themed builds (`game/assembler/worlds/Themes.luau`) put identity on the side bands and
  the background: a villain city, Namek, the Dressrosa coliseum, Konoha, the Eclipse, Hueco Mundo /
  the palace, Shibuya and the Shadow Monarch corridor.
* Each has 5 pads, a boss stage and bounded lighting.

### 16. Egg pipeline
* `EggPipeline.luau` builds 40 character-specific eggs from ellipsoid shells plus composable
  motifs (hair, masks, weapons, auras, sigils), 1,857 parts in total.
* Each egg carries its character's identity.

### 17. Approved DS eggs
* Rengoku, Akaza, Muzan and Yoriichi were moved **unchanged**; the test compares descendant counts
  with the baseline.
* Tanjiro's egg is provisional (none existed). Giyu is archived.

### 18. Egg spawns & pickup
* 45 spawns at `SAE_World.Worlds.<W>.Eggs.<Name>Spawn`, each with `Visual` and `PromptAnchor`.
* The server creates the prompt (hold 0.35 s, range 11). Each spawn is placed inside its own world
  (tested).

### 19. Carry & drop
* Carry limit 1. The egg is shown above the carrier's head.
* DROP is only possible while unsafe. A dropped egg expires after 30 s (provisional) and returns
  to its spawn.

### 20. Auto-secure
* The server's 10 Hz safe-zone loop secures the egg the instant the carrier is inside.
* There is **no** RequestSecure/SecureEgg remote; the forbidden names are asserted absent.

### 21. Hatch
* 60 s, using a server `hatchAt` timestamp saved in the profile.
* Clients render timers with `GetServerTimeNow()`; there are no per-second remotes.

### 22. Open, reveal, Index
* `RequestOpenEgg` consumes the egg before granting the unit (no duplication path).
* The reveal shows the real rig in a ViewportFrame. Index discovery is recorded on open only.

### 23. Units
* Every unit has a GUID, and duplicates are allowed. Eggs take 0 slots.
* The inventory is capped at 400 (provisional) as abuse protection.

### 24. Farms
* 7 farms in **one** perimeter-fenced safe zone with a "FARM SAFE ZONE" arch. Per-plot fences,
  bags, pads and old labels were stripped.
* Each farm has kerbs, an owner plate, a treadmill with its sign, a farm sign and a Characters folder.

### 25. Farm slots
* 5 initial slots, up to 10 (6 farm levels).
* Upgrades are made at the physical **farm sign** or the Upgrade menu, both running the same
  server function. Prompts on other players' farms are hidden locally and rejected by the server.

### 26. Farm characters
* Equipped units spawn as rigs that **wander inside their own plot** (Humanoid MoveTo) in one shared
  loop, with collision groups so they don't block players.

### 27. Income
* Base $/s = world base × slot factor, +35 % per level (provisional).
* Paid every second to online players only (**no offline income**).

### 28. Character upgrades
Levels 1–10. The server computes the cost and the new income; the client sends only the unit uid.

### 29. Equip Best
Ranks purely by actual $/sec (`Profile.equipBest`), as unit-tested.

### 30. Sell
* The Rem kiosk supports multi-select (tap cards), select all or none, and sorting by Value,
  $/sec, World or Name.
* The server validates every uid and computes the payout (current $/s × 45, provisional).

### 31. Treadmill (replaces the Boxing Bag)
* Speed is earned **only** while the owner is inside the TrainZone, grounded and moving; the belt
  pushes back at 14 studs/s.
* 10 visually evolving levels controlled by `TierMin`/`TierMax`, and a physical upgrade sign
  showing level, gain, next gain and cost.

### 32. Movement
WalkSpeed = 16 + (110 − 16) × (1 − e^(−(Speed + 20) × trail / 1000)), computed on the server
(provisional). See §47 for the balance risk.

### 33. Trails — data
Exactly x1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5, 5, 7, 10, 14, 20 (asserted at load time and in the tests).
One can be equipped at a time and they never stack. Cash prices are provisional and Robux ids are nil.

### 34. Trails — VFX (per video V01)
* A flat ribbon between two foot-height attachments, widening with age (WidthScale 0.25 → 1),
  with a long lifetime and a gradient fading at the tail.
* Shown only while moving; the aura rate scales with speed.
* Tier families: wisps → streaks → embers → lightning arcs → stars → shadow flames + rings → halo.
  The top tiers cycle their colours, and the top tier adds a second, layered ribbon.
* Only built-in textures are used.

### 35. Makima Trail Shop
A striped-awning kiosk with the Makima vendor (stand-in) and a local ProximityPrompt that opens
the Trail Shop menu. Buying and equipping are validated by the server.

### 36. Rem Sell stand
Same kiosk construction, with Rem (popular, female, not one of the 45) as the vendor.

### 37. Bosses
* One boss per world, driven by one shared state machine at 6 Hz.
* A boss reacts 1 s after a pickup (Bleach 1.4 s, Solo Leveling 1.2 s) and targets the carrier
  closest to the safe zone.
* A catch inside its world returns the egg plus knockback (22 studs, provisional); a catch outside
  its world makes the carrier drop the egg.
* Bosses never enter the safe zone. Walk speeds are 26 → 58 by world (provisional).

### 38. Seated bosses
Shigaraki, Yhwach and Sung Jin-Woo start in a throne pose and rise when the chase starts.

### 39. PvP
* The server gives everyone a Bat.
* Range (8), cone and cooldown are checked on the server. A hit knocks the carrier's egg loose,
  with a knockback event.

### 40. Rounds
* A timestamp-driven loop: GATE (7 s, blocker plus countdown board), OPEN (293 s), RESET.
* At RESET, players outside the safe zone return home and the eggs and bosses reset. The HUD timer
  and the gate board read `PhaseEndsAt`.

### 41. Persistence
* DataStore profiles with UpdateAsync **session locking**, retries, autosave (90 s), save on
  leave, and BindToClose.
* An in-memory fallback applies when API access is off, with a loud warning. The schema is
  versioned with a migrate step.

### 42. Monetization
* `MarketplaceConfig` has all ids nil. The Shop shows COMING SOON and the server refuses nil ids.
* `ProcessReceipt` is idempotent, with receipt ids stored in the profile (the last 60).

### 43. Remote security
* The client sends intent only. The Remotes gateway validates argument types, applies per-player
  rate limits and runs one mutation at a time per player.
* A dev command surface exists only in Studio or for whitelisted players.

### 44. Production UI
Built from the UI Lab design system: the same components, theme, chunky buttons, menus and art
atlases.
* **Removed:** all demo/sample data and the preview panel. The build fails if demo text leaks in.
* **Added:**
  * a round timer, a Trail Shop, a Sell menu and a real-model hatch reveal;
  * 9-world Index tabs with locked silhouettes, toasts and a Studio-only dev panel.
* The carry text is exactly "Reach the Farm Safe Zone!"
* Layout was reviewed at 1280×720 and 844×390 (`docs/screenshots/production`).

### 45. Client systems
* UIController (from the UI Lab), with an Action event and `Bind` for template clones.
* ClientState mirrors the server snapshot. ViewportService handles real models and silhouettes.
* HudApp, MenusApp (keyed list reconciliation, so viewports are not rebuilt on every snapshot),
  WorldClient and FxClient.

### 46. Lighting & zone FX
* Local lighting profiles per world are tweened on entry. The approved AkazaNight values are
  carried over from the legacy script.
* The DS river flow, compass pulse, lantern flicker and proximity lights were ported from the
  legacy AkazaZoneVFX.
* Treadmill ring spin, belt stripes and pulses animate only within 160 studs of the camera.

### 47. Balance (provisional) and risks
* Full tables are in `docs/generated/BALANCE_TABLES.md`, generated from the code.
* **Risk — needs an Owner decision:** with the WalkSpeed cap at 110, trails above x5 add little
  once a player has trained about 500 Speed (x10 and x20 both hit the cap). Options: raise the
  cap, scale `Softness` with the trail, or apply part of the trail bonus after the cap.
* **Risk:** boss walk speeds of 26–58 are below fast players' speed. This is intended for early
  worlds, but may make late bosses easy.

### 48. Performance strategies
* Shared loops instead of per-object scripts: bosses at 6 Hz, the safe zone at 10 Hz, one wander
  loop, the income tick at 1 Hz.
* Dormant idle bosses and a bounded number of lights per world.
* Treadmill tiers are hidden rather than duplicated. FX animate only near the camera.
* ViewportFrames are reused, with no per-second remotes.
* Still to profile in Studio: part count (8,940 builder parts plus the DS environment) on mobile.

### 49. Asset import & sanitization
Every reused model was sanitized: scripts, remotes, bindables, tools, unknown configurations and
suspicious attributes removed (`docs/IMPORT_AUDIT.md` §1). The place test asserts there are no
scripts, remotes or tools in Workspace or the asset folders.

### 50. Stand-ins
* 40 characters, 8 bosses and 2 vendors use original R6 stand-ins with hair styles, palettes and
  signature props. They are flagged `StandIn=true` and listed for replacement.
* The DS five use the real Last CP rigs.

### 51. Animations

premium animations (OWNER ACTION).
